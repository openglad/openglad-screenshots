THE FINDING (issue #305)
------------------------
1. The AI side gets exactly ONE bot, for every FILL setting, in both sports.
   FAIR, STRONG and BRUTAL change only how strong that single bot is — never
   how many bodies walk on. A 1-a-side "soccer match" and a 1-a-side
   "basketball game" is what a solo host is offered today.
2. FILL does not even reliably change the strength: soccer's bot is the same
   max_hp 153 at FAIR, STRONG and BRUTAL; basketball only moves at BRUTAL
   (134 -> 189). The level solver's ladder is coarser than the wheel.
3. The mechanism is the headcount rule, not a bug in the wheel:
   packs/core/lib/lineup.lua FILL_PERCENT = {WEAK 75, FAIR 100, STRONG 125,
   BRUTAL 150} scales POWER only, and
   campaigns/modes/packs/modes.core/lib/mode_match.lua:300-305
     local solved_size = squad_size            -- 5, the shipped squad table
     if <matched> then
       solved_size = og.min(matched_size, squad_size)
     end
   clamps the squad to `matched_size`, the D34 min-roster headcount — i.e. to
   the human side's headcount. One human => one bot, whatever the wheel says.
   The un-matched path (mode_match.lua:381-401) would have fielded the legacy
   FIVE.
4. FILL also writes the LOCAL seat's own band (campaign_picker.lua turn_fill,
   "Yours too." in the toast), so turning the wheel up does not widen the gap
   in the human's favour either.

HONEST LIMIT
------------
The text protocol's `state` exposes family/hp/max_hp but NOT a walker's level,
and the spawned bots are unnamed so census's `named` array (which does carry a
level) is empty. max_hp above is therefore the observable strength proxy, not
the level integer. For scale, a hand-built crew member of the same families
reads (openglad_text --protocol --campaign modes --level 820 --team <F>
--team-level <L>, then `census`):
  family 2: L1 118, L2 142, L3 166, L4 190
  family 3: L1 112, L2 124, L3 136, L4 148
The spawned bots' 153 / 134 / 189 do not sit on those ladders because the fill
path also calls set_difficulty(level), so the two numbers are not directly
comparable — the COUNT is the claim this file makes; the strength column is
supporting colour.

METHOD
------
Same shape as the BEFORE pass: drive the interactive picker to set the arena
and the FILL wheel, then GO! and read `census` over the JSON protocol. The
route changed because the Camp's MATCH SETUP page is gone: the wheel now
lives on the wizard's TEAMS step, reached through Team Build item 13.

Per-run stdin, with the ordinals spelled out ($G = game row, $A = arena row,
$R = the FILL row on the TEAMS step, $STEP = what to do to the wheel):

  1            Begin New Game
  <blank>      accept the generated company name
  5            Campaign Select -> Multiplayer Arenas
  13           Team Build -> Setup            (opens SETUP: GAME)
  $G           GAME step  -> 1 TEAM DEATHMATCH, 2 CAPTURE THE FLAG,
                             5 SOCCER, 6 BASKETBALL      (advances to ARENA)
  $A           ARENA step -> 1 = the first arena of that game; this sets the
                             level AND runs the deal, then advances to TEAMS
  $STEP        TEAMS step -> nothing (keep the dealt word),
                             "$R-" one wheel step back, "$R" one step forward,
                             "$R" "$R" two steps forward
  0            leave the wizard
  12  12       Lineup (read the bands: proof the FILL landed), back
  6            GO!
  census / state / quit / 8 / 6

  $R is 1 on a two-side arena and 2 on a four-side arena, because the TEAMS
  step grows a SIDES row above FILL when the arena authors more than two
  sides (SPEC D10). 300 THE CIRCLE is a four-side arena; that is why its runs
  press row 2.

WHAT CHANGED
------------
1. Above FAIR now buys BODIES on the two ball games: STRONG = H+1, BRUTAL =
   H+2, where H is the human side's headcount. On the base tree all six ball
   runs read [1,1,...] — one bot at every setting.
2. The shipped DEFAULT changed too, and that is the decision worth the
   maintainer's eye: a fresh soccer or basketball arena now DEALS STRONG, so
   the reporter's solo game fields two bots with no knob touched. One "<" tap
   on the FILL row restores the old FAIR 1v1, and the save keeps the choice.
3. Nothing else moved. 300 (four authored sides) and 500 (two) answer the
   same counts at FAIR, STRONG and BRUTAL that they answer at rest — the rule
   is ball-arena-only, by the campaign's own `bodies` fact.
4. The UI count and the sim agree. Every ball run's TEAMS step printed
   "TEAM 2 GREEN  n BOTS" with the same n the census then reported, and the
   LINEUP page showed the same [FILL: ...] word on the band.

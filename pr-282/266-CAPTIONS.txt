    Rendered frame 158 (= sim tick 158) of that run, 3x nearest-neighbour.

after-saffron-named-outline-closeup.png
    7x crop of the same frame around him (crop=128:80:47:58). The white halo
    around the thief on the left is palette index 7 = OUTLINE_NAMED
    (include/openglad/core/constants.h); the un-haloed walker on the right is
    the viewer's own hero.

How the frame was found, and why it is Saffron and not some other sprite:
the demo writes 8-bit indexed BMPs, so the frames were scanned for palette
index 7 directly (<scratch>/266/scan7.py). On this tree, frames 134-170 each
carry a 31-40 pixel index-7 cluster tracking one walker across the viewport;
every other frame of the 400-frame run has 6 index-7 pixels or fewer (static
HUD specks). Saffron is the only named unit in the level
(after-scen17-roster.txt: exactly one record with a non-empty name), and
walker::compute_outline draws OUTLINE_NAMED only for a BIT_NAMED walker on a
team other than the viewer's, so the outlined walker in these frames can only
be him.

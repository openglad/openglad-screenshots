Issue #266 -- AFTER, the NAMING half in pixels (recaptured 2026-09-07).
The four boss-camera PNGs above cannot show OUTLINE_NAMED: the highlight is
viewer-relative and the boss camera's viewer IS the boss. These two frames
come from a PLAYER-focus run of the same demo, same seed, where the camera's
viewer is a hero on team 0:

  nix develop -c env SDL_VIDEODRIVER=dummy SDL_AUDIODRIVER=dummy \
    SDL_RENDER_DRIVER=software OPENGLAD_DEMO_GRID=1x1 OPENGLAD_DEMO_SEED=42 \
    OPENGLAD_DEMO_CAMPAIGN=gladiator OPENGLAD_DEMO_SCENARIOS=17 \
    OPENGLAD_DEMO_TEAM_SIZE=4 OPENGLAD_DEMO_CAPTURE_FOCUS=player \
    OPENGLAD_DEMO_CAPTURE_START=0 OPENGLAD_DEMO_CAPTURE_EVERY=1 \
    OPENGLAD_DEMO_CAPTURE_LIMIT=400 OPENGLAD_DEMO_MAX_FRAMES=1000 \
    ./build/ci-test/openglad_demo

after-saffron-named-outline.png
    Rendered frame 158 (= sim tick 158) of that run, 3x nearest-neighbour.
    Saffron walks through the hero's viewport wearing the OUTLINE_NAMED
    highlight the loader sets from the new 12-byte name field; the level-5
    guild thief a few tiles right of him carries none.

after-saffron-named-outline-closeup.png
    7x crop of the same frame around him (crop=128:80:47:58). The white halo
    is palette index 7 = OUTLINE_NAMED (include/openglad/core/constants.h);
    the unnamed thief at the bottom right of the crop is the contrast.

How the frame was found, and why it is Saffron and not some other sprite:
the demo writes 8-bit indexed BMPs, so the frames were scanned for palette
index 7 directly (<scratch>/266/scan7.py). On the FIXED tree, frames 134-162
each carry a 31-40 pixel index-7 cluster tracking one walker. On the UNFIXED
level bytes (scen17.fss reverted to d25249f3, og_builtin_campaigns recomposed,
same command) no frame in the whole 327-frame run carries a cluster larger
than 8 pixels -- the specks that remain are static HUD pixels. Saffron is the
only named unit in the level (after-scen17-roster.txt: exactly one record with
a non-empty name), and walker::compute_outline draws OUTLINE_NAMED only for a
BIT_NAMED walker on a team other than the viewer's, so the outlined walker in
these frames can only be him.

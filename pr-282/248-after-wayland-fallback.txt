back without setting SDL_LOGGING). 'software' is the load-bearing line:
SDL's Wayland backend installs no window framebuffer, so the software
renderer -- the one that always rescues X11 -- is unreachable there.

invisibly, and the game reaches the intro (t20) and main menu (t30).
